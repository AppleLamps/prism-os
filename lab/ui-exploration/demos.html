Indirect demos

Every demo page in this repo, from one server: each page compiles a UI into one fixed numeric program, and JavaScript only records input, runs the program, and copies its outputs onto the page.

Original repo demos

IndirectOS

index.html

A desktop with four windows, a dock and a Photos app, where focus, stacking, dragging, resizing, window buttons, springs and the photo viewer all run in one compiled program.

Try

  • Drag a title bar, resize a window from its corner, and use the three window buttons; a closed window comes back from the dock.
  • In Photos, click a photo to open it, then use the arrow controls.

Hard attend walkthrough

hard-attend.html

Reading one of three items with a query: the highest score picks the item, and three ways of reading it are shown side by side.

Try

  • Move the query slider or pick a preset, and compare the one-hot gather, the direct gather and the softmax gather.
  • Drag the sharpness slider to see the softmax result approach the exact one.

Generative fields

lab/generative-fields/demo.html

One 16-slot layout program that becomes a grid, a hero layout, a mosaic, a list or a filmstrip purely from numbers, with interpolation between layouts repaired by the program's own validity outputs.

Try

  • Click Grid, Hero, Mosaic, List or Filmstrip, then drag the scrubber or press play, and compare the naive stage with the repaired one.
  • Press Generate for searched layouts, or click a tile to move the hero there.

spring-bank: predicted keyframes

lab/ui-exploration/spring-bank/demo.html

Fixed 4 ms springs computed as shared matrix powers. Each click predicts 1.2 seconds of motion and hands it to Web Animations, while the page itself renders only every 300 ms.

Try

  • Click anywhere to fling the five shapes.
  • Switch Prediction between 301 separate evaluations and one batched evaluation, and read the timing in the header.

spring-bank: IndirectOS

lab/ui-exploration/spring-bank/indirect-os.html

IndirectOS with the spring bank, plus controls for the spring variant, when a new destination takes effect, the frame rate, and a 500 ms main-thread stall.

Try

  • Maximize Photos, then press "stall 500 ms" or switch to 10 fps: animations still end on time. With the original springs at 10 fps they end about a second late.
  • Compare the System Monitor with the original springs: 3,580 against 5,253 operations.
