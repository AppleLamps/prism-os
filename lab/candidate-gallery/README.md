# Candidate gallery

A gallery of 12 photos that builds three complete layouts in one program and shows the one that fits the viewport best: Cinema, a hero image with a rail of thumbnails in 2 columns beside it, Contact, a contact sheet of 4 columns and 3 rows with no hero, and Story, a hero image above thumbnails in 3 columns. Each layout's score is the smallest of how well its sizes fit, where a size times its quality scale, i.e. the reciprocal of a target size, counts up to 1, and the 7 quality scales are source cells. `emitArgmax` picks the highest score, and every thumbnail and hero rectangle reads the picked layout through the argmax's one-hot, which the compiler lowers to gather reads. A click picks the thumbnail under the pointer with `emitFirstActive`, and the focused photo is state in source cells. The page is titled Dynamic UI, the name the guide uses for this demo: resize the browser window to watch the three scores and the selected layout change, and click a thumbnail to show that photo in the hero.

It moved here from the repo root on September 17, 2026, with its tests and the parts of the root tests comparison.test.ts, oracle-trace.test.ts and gather-lowering.test.ts that used it, since the repo centers on IndirectOS. [lab/runtime-cost](../runtime-cost/README.md) measures it, [codegen](../ui-exploration/codegen/README.md) and [drag-knobs](../ui-exploration/drag-knobs/README.md) keep copies of it, and the guide's [run-every-candidate.md](../ui-exploration/guide/patterns/run-every-candidate.md) and [highest-score-tree.md](../ui-exploration/guide/patterns/highest-score-tree.md) explain its constructions.

- `bun run start:candidates`: the page
- `bun test lab/candidate-gallery`: the layout that wide, balanced and tall viewports pick, Story taking over before the frame gets very narrow, clicks reaching the focus state, thumbnails keeping their slots after focus changes, and finite, bounded geometry at tiny sizes (candidate-gallery.test.ts); the circuit's rounding points are exactly its 14 boolean atoms (comparison.test.ts); the compiled program matches the reference evaluator over a scripted trace of resizes, clicks and tiny viewports (oracle-trace.test.ts); and 26 layout selection reads lower to gather reads (gather-lowering.test.ts)
- `candidate-gallery.ts`: the circuit, the machine and the page's rendering. `candidate-gallery.html` and `candidate-gallery.css`: the page, which shows IndirectOS's photos
