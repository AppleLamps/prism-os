# Learned gallery

A photo layout drawn by a small network whose coefficients are the program's source cells. For each of 20 slots, features of the slot's position that are fixed while building, i.e. the position and its sine and cosine at 4 frequencies, go through two ReGLU layers of 6 units to the slot's left, top, width and height. Training steps the coefficients with Adam, using gradients from `circuit.backpropagateReference`, until the rectangles match the target rectangles. On the page you edit a sketch of up to 12 rectangles, one per photo: drag a rectangle to move it, drag its corner to resize it, click empty space to add one, or remove the selected one with Delete or the Delete button. Training continues while you edit, so a photo follows its rectangle, and the page shows the loss, the step count and a chart of the loss under the photos.

It moved here from the repo root on September 16, 2026, since the repo now centers on HandwiredOS and its runtime. [lab/reusable-layout](../reusable-layout/README.md) also keeps a network's parameters in source cells, and feeds it features that change at runtime.

October 13, 2026: the page was rebuilt as one of the demos that `bun start` serves. The sketch starts from one of three presets, Story (10 rectangles), Cinema (9) and Contact sheet (11), drawn for a stage that is 5 wide and 4 tall, and each leaves its last cell empty so that a click has room to add a rectangle. An edit continues training from the current weights for 500 more steps after the last change, where the page before stopped training during a drag and reset the weights at the release. A preset and the Train again button start over from the seeded weights. Training runs 600 steps a second at any frame rate: 10 steps in a frame at 60 Hz and 5 at 120 Hz, at most 20 steps per frame, and at most as many as fit in 6 ms at the measured cost of a step. A delete removes the photo paired with the deleted rectangle, through the new optional `outputs` field of the target in trainable-geometry.ts, which marks the outputs in use. After the first 500 steps the loss is 2.97e-7 for Story, 1.44e-7 for Cinema and 1.25e-8 for Contact sheet, the same digits in Bun 1.4.3 and in headless Chrome 155. The sketch is edited with a mouse or a finger. No key selects or moves a rectangle, so the Tab key doesn't stop at the sketch. The network, the loss and the optimizer are unchanged: the program is still 6,320 operations in 8 layers with 244 weights.

The network alone: fits of a product layout, a layout absent from the product layouts and an irregular masonry layout, the relation loss on a rough grid drag, reset, adding and deleting rectangles, the limit of 12 rectangles, and a fit of an unseen sidebar grid. The page's editor, run frame by frame without the DOM: e.g. each preset's first 500 steps, a drag that keeps training and keeps each photo with its rectangle, the same number of steps per second at 60 Hz and 120 Hz, a finger's tap that adds a rectangle, and the numbers in the page's static text
