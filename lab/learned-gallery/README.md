# Learned gallery

A photo layout drawn by a small network whose coefficients are the program's source cells. For each of 20 slots, features of the slot's position that are fixed while building, i.e. the position and its sine and cosine at 4 frequencies, go through two ReGLU layers of 6 units to the slot's left, top, width and height. Training steps the coefficients with Adam, using gradients from `circuit.backpropagateReference`, until the rectangles match the target rectangles. On the page you edit a sketch of up to 12 rectangles, one per photo: drag a rectangle to move it, drag its corner to resize it, click empty space to add one, or remove the selected one with Delete or the Delete button. Training continues while you edit, so a photo follows its rectangle, and the page shows the loss, the step count and a chart of the loss under the photos. Edges of the sketch that are nearly on one line are fitted as one line, so the photos line up although the sketch is drawn by hand, and the "Line up edges" slider sets how near that is.

It moved here from the repo root on September 16, 2026, since the repo now centers on HandwiredOS and its runtime. [lab/reusable-layout](../reusable-layout/README.md) also keeps a network's parameters in source cells, and feeds it features that change at runtime.

October 13, 2026: the page was rebuilt as one of the demos that `bun start` serves. The sketch starts from one of three presets, Story (10 rectangles), Cinema (9) and Contact sheet (11), drawn for a stage that is 5 wide and 4 tall, and each leaves its last cell empty so that a click has room to add a rectangle. An edit continues training from the current weights for 500 more steps after the last change, where the page before stopped training during a drag and reset the weights at the release. A preset and the Train again button start over from the seeded weights. Training runs 600 steps a second at any frame rate: 10 steps in a frame at 60 Hz and 5 at 120 Hz, at most 20 steps per frame, and at most as many as fit in 6 ms at the measured cost of a step. A delete removes the photo paired with the deleted rectangle, through the new optional `outputs` field of the target in trainable-geometry.ts, which marks the outputs in use. The sketch is edited with a mouse or a finger. No key selects or moves a rectangle, so the Tab key doesn't stop at the sketch. The rebuild left the network, the loss and the optimizer as they were: the program is 6,320 operations in 8 layers with 244 weights. The loss and the training loop changed later that day, see the next paragraph.

Later on October 13, 2026, the photos were made to line up, after Cheng asked for "a bigger penalty or adjustable one for learned gallery for grid being unaligned". Measured on the page's stage at a 1440 px window, 538 by 430 px, a line of photos ended up to 0.36 px apart for a preset as it loads, about 1 px apart after a rectangle was dragged 5 to 11 px off its place, and neither on its line nor on its rectangle after a drag of 22 px. Three changes:

- **The loss.** It had a term that pulled each output edge toward a value shared with the sketch's edges near it, squared, with a strength of 8. A squared pull is a compromise with the pull toward the rectangle as drawn, so it left the dragged photo off its line and moved the line's other photos 4 to 5 px toward it, and a strength large enough to close the rest kept Cinema from finding its layout in 500 steps. Now `alignRectangles` in trainable-geometry.ts moves the sketch's edges that are within a distance of the next one onto one line, and the loss is only how far each output rectangle is from the result: the mean Huber loss over left, top, width and height, where width and height count twice, since the network otherwise fits positions sooner than sizes and leaves right and bottom edges off their lines. Left edges are compared with left edges, and likewise right, top and bottom ones. The line is where the rectangles edited longest ago have their edge, so the photo of the rectangle that was just dragged goes back to its row and no other photo moves. The distance is 3% of the stage as the page loads, 16 px across at that window, and the page's "Line up edges" slider sets it from off, where the photos follow the sketch as drawn, to 6%. A preset's edges are equal or at least 9% apart, so the slider changes no preset.
- **The weight decay** went from 0.000_02 to 0.000_000_2. It is added to the gradient, where Adam gives it as much say as a small gradient of the loss, and it held the photos 1.4 to 1.8 px from the sketch. The old loss term hid that by counting grouped edges about 8 times. Without any decay, units whose gate has closed stay shut: over 200 random edits in a row, 2 to 4 of the first layer's 6 units ended shut in 5 of 6 runs, against at most 1 with the small decay.
- **The training loop.** Adam at a fixed learning rate doesn't come to rest once the loss is low: after Cinema's first 500 steps, 10 more steps raised the loss up to 93 times, and on the page six small drags in a row made the photos jump by up to 14 px. The page's frames now train with `trainOrUndo`: when a frame's steps leave the loss higher than it was before them, the weights and Adam's moments go back, and the learning rate halves, down to 1/64 of its value. A frame that is kept multiplies the rate by 1.5, up to its value. The rate stays as it is when the sketch changes. Setting it to full at each change made every frame of a drag after a rest an undone one, with the photo standing still, and plain steps in those frames threw the other photos up to 256 px off.

In the same 60 cases, the largest move of a photo's edge between two frames in the last 250 of the 500 steps was 15 px before and is 0.34 px now, the loss rose by 2% or more in some frame of 16 cases before and of none now, and the other photos moved 3.9 px in the middle case of a drag of 11 px before and 0.01 px now. While a rectangle of Story is dragged 70 px in half a second with Line up off, so that its photo has no line to stay on, the photo is at most 3.5 px behind and the other photos move by at most 1.9 px, where the photo was 12 px behind and the others up to 10 px off before. After the first 500 steps the loss is 9.55e-9 for Story, 2.64e-10 for Cinema and 4.32e-11 for Contact sheet, and the largest error of a photo's left, top, width or height is 0.029%, 0.006% and 0.002% of the stage, where it was 0.15%, 0.06% and 0.02%. Among 200 variants of Story's proportions, every line of photos is within 0.25 px after 500 steps in 131, against 1 before, so a preset no longer has to be picked for how well it fits. One training step costs what it did, 215 µs in Bun against 219 µs, and 224 µs in the page's frames. In 6 simulated sessions of 200 random edits, an edit ended more than 5 px from where the same sketch fitted from the seeded weights puts the photos in at most 1% of the edits, against 33% to 45% before.

What was tried and not kept: a larger strength of the old term (32 to 512), pulls between output edges, squared and absolute, and an absolute pull toward the shared value. At their best these line up a rough drag within about 0.2 px, but they slow the first fit or leave edges that are a few percent apart pulled part of the way. For the jumps: a learning rate that falls over an edit's 500 steps, AMSGrad, a larger epsilon and a slower second moment, which slow the first fit, make the photos jump when the rate returns, or only delay the jumps.
