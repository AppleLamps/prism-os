The other demos’ programs are wired by hand. This one is the same kind of program, but its 244 weights are trained instead: backpropagation adjusts them until the photos match the sketch.

loss after steps

The loss measures how far the photos are from the sketch, and each training step changes the weights a little to lower the loss. Each change of the sketch gets more steps, at steps a second, and one step takes about on this device. The program is operations in layers, and the weights are its only input.
