Loss after steps

The loss measures how far the photos are from the sketch, and each training step changes the weights a little to lower the loss. Edges of the sketch that are closer to each other than the Line up distance, a share of the stage’s width or height, count as one line, so the photos line up although the sketch was drawn by hand. Each change of the sketch gets more steps, at up to steps a second, and one step takes about on this device. The program is operations in layers, and the weights are its only input.
